# Provenance: Diversity-generating retroelement RT activity

Task identifier: `C_dgr`
Directory: `tasks/taskC_dgr/`
Status: **BLOCKED, NOT BUILT**

Blocked by the Gate G1 ruling of 2026-09-30. `raw/` is empty and stays empty. No
`curated.csv` exists and none is planned.

## Why

No cross-RT DGR activity panel is established to exist. The quantitative DGR literature
concentrates on a single enzyme, the BPP-1 bRT, and reports properties of that one element,
for example that roughly 30 percent of template adenines are converted during mutagenic
homing. A property of one element is not a comparative activity measurement across enzymes.

Two candidates were seen in search results but not opened, and are recorded here for a human
who wants to check them:

1. A high-throughput analysis of a reconstituted DGR. From its title it varies the element
   and its target rather than the reverse transcriptase, which would make it a within-element
   variant-effect set, so Task-B-shaped rather than Task-C-shaped.
2. A 2026 Nature Biotechnology paper on DGRs for programmable targeted hypermutagenesis.

If either turns out to carry a usable panel it gets its own task, in its own units.

## What was done to establish this

Literature search in this session, recorded in `reports/acquisition_plan.md` with the network
log. No primary source was opened, so the finding is that no suitable panel was *found*, which
is a weaker claim than proving none exists. It is stated that way deliberately.

## What would change this

A single publication measuring a usable number of distinct reverse transcriptases under one
assay, with the label definition stated in the paper's own units. If one is identified, this
directory is opened as its own task, with its own split and its own reported metric, and it is
never pooled with Task A, Task B, or another Task C set.

## Status in the report

This negative finding is carried into `reports/data_pipeline_report.md` as a gap, not as a
workaround. Per the brief, negative results and gaps are preserved.
